# E3.M1 extended sketch tools

**Load Demos → E3.M1 Extended Sketches** includes an exact half-round solid and a separate **Extended sketch practice** feature. The solid is a 16 mm radius semicircular profile extruded 8 mm. Edit the practice sketch to try the operations without changing the solid.

- **Arc** uses three clicks for center, start and end. Open its arrow for a three-point arc through the start, intermediate point and end. Line, Rectangle and Circle also have variants in their arrow menus. Smart Dimension groups all dimension modes. The **Major arc** option takes the complementary sweep. Endpoints, center, radius and diameter have semantic references. **Polygon** uses a center, first vertex and 3–12 sides. Its connected edges use a construction circle and equality relations. **Spline** interpolates three placed points. Hold Shift to continue at a joined endpoint with a tangent relation. Select the curve to expose its tangent handles. Apply line relations and dimensions directly to each handle, or drag its control point. The arrow also offers four-click **Control Point Spline** and **Equation Driven Curve** with explicit or parametric equations and scoped parameter completion. Equation curves display 65 evaluated samples; splines and equation curves are outside the exact extrusion profile.
- **Trim** removes the clicked interval of a line or arc between analytic intersections or between an intersection and the endpoint. Click on the horizontal practice line to the right of the vertical cutting line. **Split** keeps both pieces and joins them at the clicked point. Surviving endpoint references keep their role on the correct segment. Trim also accepts a stroke started in empty space. An isolated span is removed entirely; relations and dimensions invalidated by the trim are removed. Split retains its reference guards.
- Open **Mirror Entities** or **Sketch Pattern** and use the two Inspector tables to set entities and the reference. Press Tab to switch tables. Preselected geometry populates the first table, and a single construction line seeds the mirror reference. A yellow preview appears before Apply. Mirror creates symmetry relations. Linear and circular patterns accept scoped parameters for count and spacing. Count includes the original. Choose spacing per copy or total range. A full circular turn distributes copies without duplicating the source at 360°. The 12-instance and 1000-spacing limits are removed. The current native sketch has a general 1024-primitive and 1024-relation capacity, plus its existing coordinate and document bounds. In Select, drag from empty space around the sketch entities to select them together.
- Symmetry accepts two selected points and then a line axis. Arc/circle relations reuse the real solver. Tangency to an arc checks that the contact lies on its sweep. Existing line angle, parallel, perpendicular and equal relations remain available.

Earlier sketches expose projected lines, points and circles. Side-on sketch planes expose verified cylindrical silhouettes and their endpoints. Interactive drag solves the connected constraint group in a worker; committed geometry is checked against the full sketch.

Model and semantic tool mutations use the same checked worker and history. Exact mixed contours use OCCT arcs and an independent area, perimeter and centroid calculation. This stage retains the existing Drawing projection scope. The default sheet generator can still reject partial circular outlines. Assembly, general feature patterns and machining documentation are separate milestones.

Browser acceptance creates an arc and polygon, trims and splits, mirrors, patterns and saves/reopens. Domain acceptance checks DOF, constrained drag, dependent relations, conflicting arc tangency and independent exact solid measurements.
